# Answer Bank 6 – Behavioural (STAR)

Map each question to one of your stories. Fill `[ ]` with real details.

| Question | Story | Key line |
|---|---|---|
| Worked under pressure | Acer peak-season shipment outage | "Status every two hours, workaround same day, fix next day." |
| Learned fast | IGI diamond grading at Tecogis; FactoryOS protocols | "Read the domain, sit with users, prototype early." |
| Led people | Health Assure, led 5 developers | "Split by module, daily stand-up, I took the integration risks myself." |
| Conflict | Release with known EDI edge case at Acer | "Showed data, agreed compromise, released on time." |
| Failure / mistake | Untested schema change, slow production | "Rolled back, index, always test with production-size data now." |
| Went beyond the role | Built SSRS reports so business could self-serve; built FactoryOS | "Removed a recurring ticket type." |
| Company closure | AMG closed Sep 2026 | "Handed over documentation and code cleanly until the last day." |
| Working across cultures | Indian teams → Taiwanese PMs at Acer → global Web3 team | "Write things down, confirm understanding, overlap hours." |

## Example: led people (Health Assure)
- **S:** Corporate health platform with pharmacy and teleconsultation integrations, needed for mobile apps on a deadline.
- **T:** I led five developers and owned delivery and releases.
- **A:** Split the work by module, defined the API contracts first so the mobile teams weren't blocked, took the riskiest integrations (Apollo Pharmacy, Doc24x7) myself, reviewed every PR, and ran releases.
- **R:** Delivered [on time], with APIs consumed by the iOS and Android apps. Two of the developers [took over modules independently afterwards].

## Example: company closure (AMG)
"AMG was a Web3 company, and it closed its operations in September 2026 because of regulatory and banking issues. Until the last day I documented the system, handed over code and runbooks, and made sure nothing was left half-deployed. It taught me the value of a stable company with a long-term product, which is one reason Teradyne appeals to me."

## Teradyne values to echo
Customer focus, ownership, integrity, collaboration and innovation. Use the words "customer", "ownership" and "root cause" naturally in your stories.
