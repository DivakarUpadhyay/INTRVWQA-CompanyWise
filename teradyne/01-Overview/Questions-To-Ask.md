# Questions to Ask Them

Pick 2–3 per round. Never ask about salary in the technical rounds.

## To the hiring manager

1. What does a typical week look like? How is the time split between customer issues, new development and internal tools?
2. Which platforms does the team mostly support: UltraFLEX/UltraFLEXplus, J750, or others? And which IG-XL versions?
3. How much of the customer test-program code is still VBT, and how much is moving to .NET/C#?
4. What does success look like for this role after 3 months and after 12 months?
5. How do issues come in, and how is a customer escalation handled from first report to fix?

## To engineers (technical panel)

1. What was the most interesting crash or hang you debugged recently? What tools found it?
2. How do you reproduce customer issues when you don't have their exact tester configuration or device?
3. How is the code base split between C++ and C#, and how do they talk to each other (COM, P/Invoke, C++/CLI)?
4. What does onboarding look like: training on the tester hardware, IG-XL courses?
5. Are you on ClearCase or Git, or migrating between them?

## To HR

1. Is the working language English? How much Mandarin is needed with customers?
2. How much travel to customer sites is expected?
3. What is the bonus / profit-sharing structure and is there an ESPP?
4. What are the next steps and timeline?
