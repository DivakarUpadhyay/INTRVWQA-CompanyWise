# Teradyne Interview Process (expected)

Based on public candidate reports (Glassdoor) and the job description. No public report mentions IG-XL/VBT questions specifically, so **confirm the format with the recruiter**.

## Rounds

| # | Round | Length | Who | What they test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Recruiter / HR screen | ~30 min, phone/Teams | HR | Background, why Teradyne, work permit (Gold Card, no sponsorship needed), notice period, salary, English, travel |
| 2 | Hiring manager | 45–60 min, Teams | Engineering manager | Resume deep dive, C#/.NET, VB/Excel, debugging approach. Reports say this round is mostly technical. |
| 3 | Technical panel | 2–2.5 hrs, often on-site (Hsinchu) | 2–3 engineers, one-on-one, sometimes from other Asian sites | Live coding (OOP, C/C# basics, string/array problems), VBA, WinDbg scenario, small design question (reports include "design an ATM system" and "design a file-based database") |
| 4 | HR / final | 30 min | HR / director | Culture fit, teamwork, customer handling, offer expectations |

Data point: a June 2026 Hsinchu candidate had **two engineer one-on-ones + one HR interview, about 2–2.5 hours, relaxed and project-focused**. The main question was *"Tell me about your project experience and how it relates to this role."*

## Logistics checklist

- [ ] Ask the recruiter: role title and team (application engineering / software support / IG-XL development?), location (Hsinchu? Taipei?), interview language, whether there is a coding test and in what language.
- [ ] Ask whether daily work or customer contact needs Mandarin.
- [ ] Resume printed (2 copies) if on-site. Bring your Gold Card.
- [ ] Laptop ready with VS Code / Visual Studio and Excel in case they share screen for coding.
- [ ] Teams: camera, mic, quiet room, phone on silent.
- [ ] Re-read `05-Cheatsheets/` 30 minutes before.

## Salary and HR answers

- **Expected salary:** "For a senior engineer role, I'm looking at around TWD 150k a month, but I'm flexible depending on the total package and the bonus structure." Teradyne usually pays an annual bonus plus profit sharing and stock purchase (ESPP), so ask about them.
- **Current status:** "My last company, AMG, closed its operations in September 2026, so I'm available to start immediately."
- **Work permit:** "I hold a Taiwan Employment Gold Card, so no sponsorship is needed."
- **Travel:** "Yes, I'm comfortable travelling to customer sites in Taiwan and across Asia."
