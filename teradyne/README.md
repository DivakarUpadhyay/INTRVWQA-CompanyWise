## Recommended order

1. `01-Overview/JD-Analysis.md` – know where you are strong and where to bridge.
2. `05-Cheatsheets/IGXL-ATE-Primer.md` – the domain you have not worked in yet.
3. `02-Mock-Interview/Full-Mock-Interview.md` – read the answers, then say them in your own words.
4. `04-Coding-Practice/` – solve each drill without looking, then compare.
5. `06-Practice-App/mock-interview.html` – timed practice until every card is "confident".
6. Day before: `01-Overview/Interview-Process.md` checklist + `Questions-To-Ask.md`.

- Any personal debugging story numbers (how long it took, how many users affected). The model answers use realistic placeholders marked `[ ]`.
