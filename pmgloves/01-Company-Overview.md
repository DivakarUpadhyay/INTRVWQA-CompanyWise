# PM Gloves — Company Overview

**Legal name:** 禾寶醫療器材股份有限公司 (Precious Mountain Ent. Corp. / PM Group, 禾寶醫療集團)
**Website:** https://www.pmgloves.com
**HQ:** Taipei, Taiwan (phone +886 2-6606-6688)

## Key facts (memorise these)
- **Founded 1978** by 8 experts in chemical materials and machinery, at a time when Japanese glove makers were looking for overseas partners.
- **Vision:** "the top global disposable glove maker."
- **Six factories** in Taiwan (Tainan), China and Vietnam.
- **About 17.4 billion gloves a year**, **3,000+ staff**, sold in **100+ markets**.
- More than 30 years of making vinyl gloves.

**Segments:** medical, dental, food, general use and pets, cleanroom and biomedical, heavy industry, laboratory and pharmaceutical.

## Talking points for the interview
- It's a **manufacturer with an OEM/export business**: customers are distributors and private-label brands in the US and Europe.
- The glove industry boomed in COVID, then had a price crash from oversupply, mainly from Malaysian makers (Top Glove, Hartalega). Expect questions on competitiveness, cost and quality.
- Running 6 plants in 3 countries means **ERP, MES, production and quality data, and supply chain** across sites. This is where a software/IT profile adds value.
- Compliance matters: FDA 510(k), CE / EN 455, EN 374, ISO 13485, ASTM D6319 (nitrile) and D5250 (vinyl).
