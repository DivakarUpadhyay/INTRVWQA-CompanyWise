1. Interviews are **conversational, low difficulty, and focus on your past projects**. Have 3–4 project stories ready, told in depth.
2. They test **concepts more than tricky puzzles**. Know your fundamentals cleanly.
3. **Stability matters.** Tenure is short, so they'll probe whether you'll stay. Show commitment (Gold Card, settling in Taiwan, learning Mandarin).
4. **Salary bands are moderate.** See the salary strategy in 03.
5. Culture is top-down. Present yourself as cooperative: *"I'll follow the team's priorities and propose improvements with data."*
